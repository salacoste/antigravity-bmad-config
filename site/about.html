A thin layer, on purpose

This repo will never be a BMad fork. It pins, wires, and gets out of the way.

Philosophy

Everything here exists to wire BMad into Antigravity natively. Nothing here adds methodology of its own.

What this repo is

A thin layer over a pinned upstream. It wires BMad into Antigravity natively — a setup script, an orchestrator plugin, and presets — and adds no methodology of its own.

What it will never be

A BMad fork. The pin means upstream releases never change your project under you; the thinness means every piece stays inspectable and replaceable. The BMad runtime is installer-generated, not hand-maintained.

When BMad changes, the template changes one line — .bmad-version — plus whatever make smoke-test catches. Not a rewrite.

Contributing

Ground rules

  • Generated directories are read-only for contributors: _bmad/, _bmad-output/, .agent/, .agents/skills/.
  • Preset changes go in presets/** — the source. _bmad/custom/ is installer output.
  • The pin moves only with a passing make smoke-test.
  • Don't reference the removed v6 shim skill names — bmad-create-story and bmad-dev-story are gone in BMad v7.
