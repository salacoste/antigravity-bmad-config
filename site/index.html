The pivot, briefly

Antigravity sunset workflows on 2026-10-19, and BMad 6 dropped .bmad-core — the two things the v1 approach stood on. BMad ≥ 6 installs its Antigravity targets natively, so a transposition layer became dead weight. This template keeps only what upstream doesn't ship: an orchestrator, presets, and a pin you control.
